About the club

We are the Physics & Astronomy Club (PAC) at Mongol Aspiration International School in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia. Our aim is to give students a starting point in astronomy and physics, and a place where questions can be asked until they become understanding.

Founded by Galbadrakh Ulziibold on 14 November 2024, our acronym PAC takes quiet inspiration from Pac-Man: moving forward step by step with steady curiosity, working through one question, one observation, and one idea at a time. You do not need to know all the answers to begin.

Club handouts

Short, self-contained handouts introducing core ideas in physics and astronomy through physical intuition, worked examples, remarks, and graded problems ([1] warm-up to [3] challenge). Every sheet can be read directly on the web or printed for club sessions.

What we explore

Physics

How do we describe the world around us? We start with questions about motion, forces, energy, gravity, and light, connecting mathematical ideas to clear physical intuition.

Astronomy

What can we learn by looking up? From the motion of the Moon and planets above Mongolia to distant stars and black holes, astronomy gives us a way to explore the universe through physics.

Learning together

A good question is a starting point. We value working through problems step by step, checking limiting cases, sharing ideas, and making subtle concepts simple to understand.
