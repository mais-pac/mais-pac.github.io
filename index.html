Physics · Astronomy · Curiosity

The Physics & Astronomy Club at Mongol Aspiration International School. Its aim is to give students a starting point in astronomy, and a place where questions can be asked until they become understanding.

Founded

On 14 November 2024, Galbadrakh Ulziibold founded the Physics & Astronomy Club at Mongol Aspiration International School.

Purpose

The club gives students the starting point that is often missing. It is a community where curiosity is welcome, questions have room to develop, and a student who knows nothing about astrophysics can grow step by step toward something real.

The aim is to help students see the world the way the founder now sees it: the sky is not just a backdrop, and the rules behind it can be learned.
