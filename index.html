About the club

We are the Physics & Astronomy Club at Mongol Aspiration International School in Mongolia. Our aim is to give students a starting point in astronomy, and a place where questions can be asked until they become understanding.

Founded by Galbadrakh Ulziibold on 14 November 2024, the club is for students who want to look a little closer, work through an idea, and understand how things work. You do not need to know all the answers to begin.

What we explore

Physics

How do we describe the world around us? We start with questions about motion, forces, energy, and light, and connect mathematical ideas to physical understanding.

Astronomy

What can we learn by looking up? From the motion of planets to stars and black holes, astronomy gives us a way to explore the universe through physics.

Learning together

A good question is a starting point. We value working through problems step by step, sharing ideas, and making difficult concepts easier to understand.
