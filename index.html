A place to ask questions

We are the Physics & Astronomy Club at Mongol Aspiration International School in Mongolia. Our aim is to give students a starting point in astronomy, and a place where questions can be asked until they become understanding.

Founded by Galbadrakh Ulziibold on 14 November 2024, the club is for anyone who wants to look a little closer, work through an idea, and understand how things work. You do not need to know all the answers to begin.
