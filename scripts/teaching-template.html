Club handouts

Short, self-contained handouts introducing core ideas in physics and astronomy through physical intuition, worked examples, remarks, and graded problems ([1] warm-up to [3] challenge). Every sheet can be read directly on the web or printed for club sessions.

I wrote this series to be the resource I wish I had when I first began studying astronomy, and I hope it helps other students develop both their understanding of physics and their curiosity about the universe.

Physics

  • P1 Physical Reasoning & Estimation Units, dimensional analysis, limiting cases, and order-of-magnitude thinking Handout Solutions
  • P2 Motion, Forces & Conservation Kinematics, free-body diagrams, energy, and momentum Handout Solutions
  • P3 Gravity, Orbits & Waves Universal gravitation, Kepler’s laws, orbital energy, and Doppler shifts Handout Solutions

Astronomy

  • A1 The Night Sky & Celestial Motion Angular measure, celestial coordinates, sidereal time, and the sky over Ulaanbaatar Handout Solutions
  • A2 Light, Telescopes & Stars Inverse-square flux, magnitudes, blackbody spectra, and stellar lifetimes Handout Solutions
  • A3 Relativity & Black Holes Escape speed, the Schwarzschild radius, tidal forces, and glowing accretion disks Handout Solutions
