International Physics Olympiad (IPhO)

Organized by the IPhO syllabus, topic by topic: the same structure as the IOAA curriculum above. Every topic has a lecture with worked reasoning, vector figures, an interactive experiment, ten original problems and ten fully worked solutions. (Q)-style qualitative scope is marked in the lectures where the syllabus leaves it qualitative.

  • IPhO syllabus reading copy (PDF) — supplied 2014/2015 syllabus with source note; IPhO syllabus webpage ↗
  • 24 topics · 240 original problems · 240 worked solutions

The IPhO curriculum is complete. All 24 syllabus topics now follow the IOAA handout design: a from-scratch lecture, five or more vector figures, an interactive experiment, ten graded problems and matching worked solutions (240 problems, 240 solutions). The three older introductory guides remain available as Theoretical Skills, Experimental Skills and Mathematics. For timed practice, use the IPhO past-paper archive.
