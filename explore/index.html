What we explore

Physics

How do we describe the world around us? We start with questions about motion, forces, energy, gravity, and light, connecting mathematical ideas to clear physical intuition.

Astronomy

What can we learn by looking up? From the motion of the Moon and planets above Mongolia to distant stars and black holes, astronomy gives us a way to explore the universe through physics.

Learning together

A good question is a starting point. We value working through problems step by step, checking limiting cases, sharing ideas, and making subtle concepts simple to understand.
